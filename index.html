EVIDENCE MODEL

How the XAUUSD bias is built

Gold Mine does not invent a trade from a headline. It scores released macro facts, then checks whether USD and yield markets confirm the same direction.

Fed / interest rates / guidance45
Inflation (CPI, Core CPI, PCE)24
Labour (NFP, unemployment, claims)16
Growth / activity10

“Evidence strength” measures how strongly the available inputs agree. It is not a claimed probability of a profitable trade.
