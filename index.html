EVIDENCE MODEL

What Gold Mine considers

No single headline automatically becomes a buy or sell. The model looks at all recent US releases it receives, then adds market and headline context where the evidence is clear.

Fed / rates / guidance45
Inflation (CPI, PCE, PPI)28
Labour (NFP, unemployment, claims, JOLTS)22
GDP / growth18
Consumer / retail / sentiment12
Business / PMIs / production10
Trade / housing / government / other5–9

The weights show relative macro importance, not a promised price move. “Evidence strength” measures agreement among available inputs; it is not a win probability.

FREE DATA LIMITS

What “live” means on this free setup

The calendar/news collector runs automatically on GitHub roughly every 10 minutes. GitHub can occasionally delay scheduled jobs, and free market quotes may be delayed. For institutional-style instant feeds we would eventually need a paid or dedicated realtime backend.
