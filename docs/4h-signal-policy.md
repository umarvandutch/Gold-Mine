# Gold Mine 4H signal policy

- Low-impact calendar releases and low-impact macro headlines are excluded.
- Ordinary medium/high-impact releases and directional headlines can contribute for up to 7 days.
- CPI and Federal Reserve / interest-rate evidence can contribute for up to 90 days.
- CPI/rates evidence older than 7 days is sharply down-weighted and acts only as a background regime factor.
- The Trade Plan primary execution timeframe is 4H. The macro/news model supplies direction; a 4H order block and price-structure confirmation are still required before entry.
- No paid data source is required by this policy.
